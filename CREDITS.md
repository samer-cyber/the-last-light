## Signs and clues

The vault's stencilled signs are painted by `tools/make_signs.py` with Windows' Stencil and Bahnschrift fonts. The clues (the protocols poster, the status screen, Mara's note and last words, the scratched messages, Spark's tally) are painted by `tools/make_clues.py` with Windows' Ink Free, Bahnschrift, Consolas and Comic Sans. Ima's crayon drawing was drawn by Meta's Muse Image and signed in `tools/make_clues.py`.

## Sound and voice

Sound effects, music and Spark's voice: ElevenLabs (https://elevenlabs.io). Spark's voice is "Game robot voice", designed by Samer with ElevenLabs Voice Design. The survivor's remembered voice is the ElevenLabs library voice Daniel; Ima's recording is the library voice Kavya.

Spark's mind runs on Meta's Muse Spark; speech is heard with Muse Voice Transcribe; holograms are drawn by Muse Image.
